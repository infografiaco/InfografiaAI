# Concepts — InfografiAI

## Naming status
"InfografiAI" remains the working name per the brief — treat as provisional, not final.
Note for brand book: acknowledge this is deliberate (per section 13, avoid "logo + AI
gradient" thinking) — the concept work below should stand even if the final name changes.

## Core conceptual territory: "Direction, not generation"
Every visual and verbal concept should trace back to this idea. The brand is not about
AI as spectacle — it's about the visible presence of a director's hand inside a
generative process. Concepts that fail this test should be cut, however visually
striking.

## Candidate concept directions

### 1. "The Construction Shot" (drawn directly from the current R&D experiment, section 9)
The USB/microchip camera-move project — structures mechanically assembling themselves
around a continuously moving camera — is a near-literal metaphor for the brand: things
being *built with intention* in real time, not conjured. This could become a recurring
visual signature (structures assembling/resolving into clarity) rather than a one-off
experiment.
**Strength:** Authentic, already proven technically, ownable.
**Risk:** Could tip into generic "tech" visual language if overused — needs restraint.

### 2. "Storyboard as artifact"
Treat the storyboard/previs/shot-breakdown itself as a brand asset, not just internal
process. Show frames, annotations, camera notes — the same way a film studio might
publish concept art. This directly operationalises "process transparency as proof"
from messaging.md.
**Strength:** Cheap to produce continuously, reinforces direction-over-generation,
differentiates from every competitor's polished-clip-only reels.
**Risk:** Needs a genuinely considered visual system for the storyboard/annotation
style itself, or it reads as behind-the-scenes filler rather than brand asset.

### 3. "Calibration / instrumentation" motif
Visual language borrowed from precision instruments, camera rigs, lens calibration,
technical measurement — NOT circuitry/neural-net imagery. Ties to Infografia's
information-design heritage (precision, clarity) while feeling distinct and avoiding
every cliché listed in section 13.
**Strength:** Directly answers "should still relate to Infografia's design heritage."
**Risk:** Needs careful execution to avoid reading as generic "engineering" branding.

## Recommended combination
(1) as the signature motion/visual metaphor, (2) as the ongoing content strategy across
every touchpoint, (3) as the underlying design-system language (grid, iconography,
typographic precision). These three reinforce each other rather than compete.

## Concepts explicitly rejected (per section 13 exclusions)
- Neon cyberpunk
- Robot / brain imagery
- Glowing circuitry
- Corporate SaaS gradient-and-blob branding
- Generic "AI sparkle" iconography
