# Positioning — InfografiAI

## Category
AI-directed creative production. Not "AI video studio," not "production company," not "AI content."
The discipline of art-directing generative AI the way a DP directs a camera — not the way you type a prompt.

## Structural position
InfografiAI is a division of Infografia — "InfografiAI, by Infografia" — not a standalone sister brand.
It inherits Infografia's 15-year credibility and enterprise/government trust, while operating its own
visual language and creative latitude for cinematic, experimental, brand-film and advertising work that
wouldn't sit comfortably next to a government report.

This is a deliberate, reviewable decision, not a default — see `differentiation.md` for the case against
a fully independent brand, and revisit if/when demand proves the division needs to graduate.

## Positioning statement
For organisations and brands who want cinematic, high-craft visual content directed with intention —
not generated at random — InfografiAI is the AI-directed production division of Infografia. We combine
15+ years of information design, motion design and film craft with deep technical command of generative
pipelines, so the work does what was previously impossible for a project at this scale — and, because the
pipeline is AI-native, does it inside a fraction of a traditional production's budget and timeline.

Capability first. Budget and speed are the proof, not the pitch.

## The one-line version
AI is the production capability. Creative direction is the product.

## What earns the position (proof, not claims)
- 15+ years, named enterprise/government clients (Home Affairs, CSIRO, Transport for NSW, Qantas, NSW
  Reconstruction Authority) — credibility a pure AI studio cannot borrow or fake.
- Hybrid pipeline fluency: C4D → ComfyUI → Higgsfield / Seedance / Kling → After Effects → traditional
  animation, sound, edit. Technical depth most "AI creatives" don't have and most agencies can't operate.
- Process transparency as a selling point: showing direction (storyboard → previs → AI transformation →
  compositing), not just a finished clip with no visible craft behind it.
- Demonstrated capability unlocked by AI-native cost structure — projects that would have needed a
  traditional-production budget are now viable, without lowering the craft bar.

## What this position explicitly rules out
- Competing on "we have access to AI models" — everyone will, soon.
- Prompt-and-post content mills, and any race-to-the-bottom on turnaround time alone.
- Generic AI aesthetic: neon cyberpunk, glowing circuitry, robot/brain imagery, corporate SaaS gradients.
- Being read as "cheaper Infografia" — it is a different creative register, not a discount tier of the
  same output. Cost efficiency is a downstream benefit of the method, never the headline.

## Open items to pressure-test in audience.yaml / messaging.md
- Confirm which audience segments respond more to "previously impossible" vs "previously unaffordable" —
  the blend ratio may differ between, say, a brand/ad client and an enterprise/government client.
- Decide how explicitly cost/speed appears in client-facing messaging vs. how much stays as an internal
  sales conversation point (recommend: capability-led headline copy everywhere; budget/speed discussed
  in pitch/proposal contexts, not on-site or in the reel).
